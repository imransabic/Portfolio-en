About the club

A place where
character is built

BJJ in your town is a club dedicated to authentic Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu — a sport with no limits on age, gender or body type.

Our philosophy is simple: technique beats strength. Whether you're here for self-defense, competition or fitness, you'll find your place on the mats.

  • Classes are taught exclusively by black and brown belt holders
  • Programs tailored for kids from age 5, beginners and competitors
  • Over 400 m² of mat space in a modern facility in the heart of your town

Programs

Something for
everyone

No matter your experience level or age, we have a program for you. All classes include drilling, sparring and conditioning.

01

BJJ Fundamentals

An introduction to jiu-jitsu — positions, basic techniques, escapes and core principles. Ideal for complete beginners.

Beginners 75 min Gi
02

Advanced Gi

Advanced techniques, complex transitions, guard passing and competition tactics for more experienced students.

Advanced 90 min Gi
03

No-Gi Grappling

No kimono — faster pace, wrestling, submission grappling and preparation for MMA and ADCC rules.

All levels 75 min No-Gi
04

Kids BJJ

A program for kids aged 5 to 14 — developing coordination, discipline, confidence and respect.

Ages 5–14 50 min Gi
05

Women's BJJ

A women-only class — a safe environment focused on self-defense and realistic attack scenarios.

Women only 70 min Gi / No-Gi
06

Open Mat

Free sparring with no structured class. Come, roll and exchange knowledge with others.

All levels 120 min Gi / No-Gi
